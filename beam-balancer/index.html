Beam Balancer

Personal · 2025 · 2025

The 1-D precursor to the Stewart platform. Linkage to firmware in two weeks.

A ball rolls on a beam tilted by a servo through a four-bar linkage. Link lengths were chosen from 2-D constraints for mechanical advantage and a compact footprint. An OpenCV tracker measures ball position and a tuned controller commands the servo through embedded C firmware.

The precursor

1-D ball balancing before the 2-D Stewart platform. I went from choosing the ideal link lengths to 3D modelling the mechanism to final code in about two weeks.

  • Python
  • C
  • PID
  • OpenCV
  • SolidWorks
