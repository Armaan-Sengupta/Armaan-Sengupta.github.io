Contact Localization

Research · RIPL · Sep 2025 – Jan 2026

A MuJoCo–ROS 2 bridge for the Franka Panda with force injection to benchmark contact localization.

Built a MuJoCo–ROS 2 bridge for the Franka Panda so existing robot missions run unchanged in high-fidelity physics, then added external force injection with live joint-torque feedback to benchmark torque-based contact localization.

  • Python
  • ROS 2
  • MuJoCo
  • Franka Panda
