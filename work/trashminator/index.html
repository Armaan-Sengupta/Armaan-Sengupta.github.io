The Trashminator

Grade 12 · Design · 2022

An autonomous surface robot that collects floating trash, built to work in swarms.

A macro surface-level aquatic cleaning robot that autonomously navigates a body of water and collects floating debris; a swarm of them could take on aquatic pollution. It began as a technological design course project and became a personal one.

The robot

My macro surface-level aquatic cleaning robot. It autonomously navigates a body of water and collects floating trash, and a swarm of them could help fight aquatic pollution. It started as a technological design course project and became a personal one I kept improving.
