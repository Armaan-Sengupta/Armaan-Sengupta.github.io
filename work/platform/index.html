Stewart Platform

Ball balancing robot · Dec 2025

A ball balancing robot: inverse kinematics, a Simscape plant, and a force-torque sensor that finds the ball.

  • Inverse kinematics to three servos
  • Simscape plant, PID and feedforward
  • Force-torque sensor finds the ball

A 3RRS parallel manipulator that balances a ball on its plate. Inverse kinematics map a desired plate plane to three servo angles; a Simscape multibody plant with a custom C contact S-function let controllers be tuned before touching hardware.

Trajectory-planned feedforward was compared against classic PID on settling and overshoot, and the ball was located with a 3-axis force-torque sensor, benchmarked against an OpenCV vision baseline.

The goal

Balance a ball on a plate. Our design is a 3RRS parallel manipulator: three degrees of freedom rather than a full Stewart platform's six, which is enough to do the same job.

Building blocks

Forward and inverse kinematics for the servo legs; a Simulink and Simscape plant, characterized against the hardware, to tune controllers before running them; trajectory-planned feedforward compared against PID; and ball position sensed by a 3-axis force-torque sensor under the plate.

  • Python
  • Simulink · Simscape
  • Kinematics
  • PID · feedforward
  • OpenCV
