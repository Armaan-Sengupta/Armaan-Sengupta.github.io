Two-Axis Gantry

Year 3 · STM32 · 2025

Interrupt-driven endstops, UART positioning and an ADC-characterized pot.

An STM32 drives two stepper axes. Interrupts on the endstops stop motion into a limit, a homing routine zeros the machine, UART sets position from a PC, and a characterized ADC lets a potentiometer position each axis precisely.

The build

An STM32 controls a two-axis machine over UART and a potentiometer, with safety stops and homing. Interrupts catch the endstops so the machine never drives past a limit, and a characterized ADC lets the potentiometer set each axis precisely. The final demo showed safe stops at the limits and a race to position the machine over a target as fast and precisely as possible.
