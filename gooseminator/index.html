The Gooseminator

First year · Team design · 2022

Sweeps an area and sorts bird eggs by whether they are an invasive or protected species.

The robot

A group project for our mechatronics course: the robot sweeps an area and sorts bird eggs by whether they belong to an invasive or a protected species. The name comes from the running Waterloo joke that the geese are out of control. Our design document covers the full engineering design process.
